SCOPE REMINDER
--------------

- Platform/India administrators: select the office before demonstrating its records.
- Location administrators: work within their assigned office.
- Project administrators: manage assigned projects/leads and existing guard duties; cannot create sibling projects, office common areas or new
local guard accounts.
- Guard duty, employee membership, project administration and reception scope are separate assignments. A project-wide lead grant does not
automatically give employee host membership in every office.
